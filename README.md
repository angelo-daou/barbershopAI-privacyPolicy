# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 7, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how the Barber Shop AI Assistant (“the Service”) handles information when you use our WhatsApp-based appointment booking service.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you communicate with the Service through WhatsApp, we may receive and process:

* Your WhatsApp phone number.
* Your WhatsApp profile name, when provided by WhatsApp.
* Messages you send to the Service.
* Information you provide during a booking, such as your name, selected barber, selected service, and appointment date and time.

We only collect information that is necessary to provide the appointment booking service.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we receive to:

* Respond to your WhatsApp messages.
* Answer questions about available barbers and services.
* Check appointment availability.
* Create and manage appointments.
* Provide customer support and improve the Service.

We do not use your information for purposes unrelated to providing or improving the Service.

## 3. AI Processing

The Service uses artificial intelligence to understand and respond to messages and assist with appointment bookings.

Messages and relevant booking information may be processed by third-party AI service providers, including Google Gemini, in order to generate responses and perform requested actions.

## 4. WhatsApp

The Service operates through WhatsApp and receives messages through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Your use of WhatsApp is also subject to WhatsApp's own terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Data Storage

Information required to manage appointments may be stored in our database, including your name, phone number, appointment details, and information necessary to associate your appointment with the relevant barber shop.

We retain this information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, manage appointments, maintain records, and comply with applicable obligations.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or transmit information to service providers that are necessary to operate the Service, including:

* Meta / WhatsApp, for messaging functionality.
* Google, for AI-powered message processing.
* Hosting, database, and infrastructure providers used to operate the Service.

These providers process information only as necessary to provide their respective services.

## 7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information handled by the Service. However, no method of transmitting or storing information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information that we hold about you.

To make a request, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
